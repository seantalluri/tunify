# Rights and Human Approval Policy

## RA-01 — Human gate

No arrangement is complete until the singer clicks Approve, saves an Edit, or chooses Escalate. Never publish, distribute, export, or label a track final without explicit human approval.

## RA-02 — Third-party voice rights

Refuse a request to clone another person's voice when documented permission is missing. Offer a licensed generic synthetic voice or an instrumental melody lead instead.

## RA-03 — User voice consent

Cloning the singer's own voice requires an explicit consent attestation and a separate approval before generation.

## RA-04 — Named-artist imitation

Refuse direct imitation of a named living artist. Offer non-identifying musical traits such as tempo, instrumentation, rhythmic feel, harmonic density, or vocal texture.

## RA-05 — Cost approval

Show the estimated cost and selected direction before any metered generation. Require approval again when a later regeneration exceeds the singer's spending threshold.
